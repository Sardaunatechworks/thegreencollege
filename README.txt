THE GREEN COLLEGE JOS — BROWSER-ONLY DEMONSTRATION

Open index.html in a modern browser. No XAMPP, PHP, MySQL or internet connection is required for the core demo.

Gallery photos are in assets/gallery. To change a title or caption, edit the cards in gallery.html.

Pages: Home, About Us, Academics, Gallery, Our Team, Admissions, School Fees, Contact Us, Student/Staff/Admin portal.

Create an account first, then sign in. Account roles and records are stored in the browser localStorage. If browser data is cleared, the demo data may be lost. Data is not shared across devices or users.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS: This is not a production school management system. Browser storage is not secure; role selection can be manipulated; passwords are not safely protected; uploaded photos remain in this browser. Never enter real student data or sensitive information. Admissions are saved locally only. Payment and receipt features are simulations; no money is collected and a generated receipt is not proof of payment.

For a real deployment, the school will need a secure server, database, server-side authentication and authorization, HTTPS, backups, privacy controls, and verified payment gateway integration.
